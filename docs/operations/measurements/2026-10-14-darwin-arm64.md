## Command-line pipeline

Commands, each measured with `/usr/bin/time -l`, from binaries built with `go build`:

    synthesize -records N -seed 1 > data.jsonl
    train -config docs/operations/benchmark-config.json -dataset data.jsonl -run-dir runs/N
    promote -run runs/N -output model.json
    evaluate -model model.json -dataset data.jsonl -partition test -output evaluation.json
